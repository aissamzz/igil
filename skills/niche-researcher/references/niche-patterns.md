# Niche Patterns Reference

Fallback design and copy patterns per niche. Used when Brave API research fails or returns no usable results.

---

## Dentists

**Colors:** `#FFFFFF` (white), `#1B4F8A` (navy), `#E8F4FD` (light blue)
**Section order:** Hero → Services → About → Reviews → Contact
**Hero style:** Clean white background, professional photo or dental imagery
**Common services:** Teeth Whitening, Invisalign, Emergency Dentistry, Dental Implants, Cleanings & Exams, Veneers
**Tone:** Clean, clinical, trustworthy. Focus on comfort and expertise.
**CTAs:** "Book an Appointment", "Schedule Online", "Call Us Today", "New Patient Special"
**Trust signals:** "Accepting New Patients", years in practice, insurance accepted

---

## Aesthetic Clinics

**Colors:** `#FFFFFF` (white), `#F5E6E0` (blush), `#C9A96E` (gold), `#1A1A1A` (near-black)
**Section order:** Hero → Treatments → About → Before/After (if images available) → Reviews → Contact
**Hero style:** Minimal, luxury feel — large white space, serif heading
**Common services:** Botox & Fillers, Laser Hair Removal, Chemical Peels, Microneedling, IV Therapy, Body Contouring
**Tone:** Premium, aspirational, modern. Emphasize results and discretion.
**CTAs:** "Book a Consultation", "View Treatments", "Reserve Your Appointment"
**Trust signals:** Licensed practitioners, FDA-approved treatments, member organizations

---

## Boutique Hotels

**Colors:** `#F5F0E8` (warm cream), `#2D4A3E` (dark green), `#8B7355` (warm brown)
**Section order:** Hero (full-screen) → Rooms → Amenities → Location → Reviews → Contact
**Hero style:** Full-width atmospheric photography, editorial headline
**Common sections:** Rooms & Suites, Dining, Spa & Wellness, Local Experiences
**Tone:** Editorial, atmospheric, refined. Story-driven.
**CTAs:** "Check Availability", "Reserve a Room", "Book Direct", "Plan Your Stay"
**Trust signals:** TripAdvisor awards, years established, unique property features

---

## Local Services

**Colors:** `#1E3A8A` (blue), `#FFFFFF` (white), `#F97316` (orange accent)
**Section order:** Hero → Services → Why Choose Us → Service Area → Reviews → Contact
**Hero style:** Bold headline with clear CTA, trust badges
**Common sections:** Services, Service Area Map, Certifications, Emergency Contact
**Tone:** Direct, reliable, local. Emphasize speed, trustworthiness, local knowledge.
**CTAs:** "Get a Free Quote", "Call Now", "Book a Service", "Available 24/7"
**Trust signals:** Licensed & insured, local to city, response time, years in business
